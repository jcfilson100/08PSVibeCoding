# Validation Brief

**Scenario:** Scenario 02

## 1 · Hypothesis
I'm testing whether CRM with 18% adoption. Built it, no one came. Figure out why adoption is so low and prototype a version that actually fits how people work

## 2 · Risk type & kill switch
- **Risk type:** Feasibility
- **Build to test it:** Functional app
- **Kill switch:** If weekly active adoption is below 50% of licensed users at the 6-month mark AND has not grown more than 5 points over the prior 60 days, trigger a go/no-go review before the next contract renewal.

## 3 · Success criteria
60% or more weekly active licensed users
60% of active users are active over 30 days

## 4 · Problem Framework
1. **Goal**, Users are engaging with the CRM and utilizing it's features, not just logging in.
2. **Problem**, Users are not even working in the CRM.
3. **Context**, As a CRM, we need salespeople to log into the CRM and log opportunities in the system as they occur.
They can access it from anywhere and need to update sales opportunities in near time.
4. **Constraints**, The CRM system can only be paid for and maintained for 1 year and if utilization does not increase and promise delivered sales improvement targets it will be shut down.
5. **Success**, Data completeness for owner, stage, and next step logs are above 60% for opportunities
6. **Explore**, How can I drive utilization and a functional application for the sales people that they will use with regularity.
