## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup. Their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

What she thought it did: Assumed a "Critical" score would trigger an actual governance action — routing, notification, a decision.
What surprised her: The score just displays a message and stops. There's no status field, no approver, no audit trail, and the scoring weights are defaults she'd never signed off on.
The gap: I built a scoring calculator; she read it as the governance decision process itself.
